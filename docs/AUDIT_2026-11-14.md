## 1. Executive Summary of Critical Audit Findings

A rigorous audit of `data/manifest.csv`, `data/manifest.json`, `public/nisar-derived/nisar_registry.json`, `src/game/worldMapData.ts`, and project documentation revealed several severe scientific and geometrical defects that undermine validity if left uncorrected:

1. **Defect A: Pre-Event Pair Mislabeled as Co-Event:** The granule assigned as `"CO_EVENT_PAIR"` (`NISAR_L2_PR_GUNW_026_098_A_016_028`) covers `2026-07-26` to `2026-08-19`. It terminated 7 days **prior** to the 2026-08-26 disaster, rendering it an inter-monsoon baseline pair, NOT a co-event interferogram.
2. **Defect B: Geometry Cross-Contamination in Swipe-Wipe:** The `"POST_EVENT_LATEST"` entry in `nisar_registry.json` claimed to represent `2026-11-11 Track 98 Frame 16 Ascending`, but its raster file (`rasuwa_nepal_nisar.png`) was identical in byte-size and SHA-256 hash to the `2026-11-08 Track 48 Frame 74 Descending` acquisition. The interactive swipe-wipe was comparing an Ascending look direction against a Descending look direction—violating the fundamental InSAR rule against cross-geometry differencing.
3. **Defect C: Uncalibrated Thumbnail Limitations:** The bundled rasters are low-resolution browse images (~100×100 px, ~13–15 KB). While suitable for qualitative spatial screening, they cannot support quantitative backscatter ($\sigma^\circ$) or millimeter phase displacement calculations.
4. **Defect D: Document Contradictions:** `README.md`, `docs/SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md`, `docs/qa/REPORT.md`, and `nisar_registry.json` contained conflicting claims regarding test counts (19 vs. 45), chunk sizes, geometry IDs, and verification statuses.
5. **Defect E: Misleading Early-Warning Dialogue:** An NPC question in `src/game/worldMapData.ts` asked *"How did NISAR detect the flood 7 days before Aug 26?"*, implying an impossible satellite early-warning capability.
6. **Defect F: Unresolved Credit Line Placeholder:** Registry files contained unfinalized credit strings with `[HUMAN: confirm credit line]`.

## 2. Detailed Technical Evidence & Root Causes

### Defect A: False Co-Event Granule
- **Granule in Registry:** `NISAR_L2_PR_GUNW_026_098_A_016_028_4000_SH_20260726T233919_20260726T233954_20260819T233918_20260819T233953_X05026_N_F_J_001`
- **Actual Coverage:** July 26, 2026, 23:39:19 UTC to August 19, 2026, 23:39:53 UTC.
- **Event Date:** August 26, 2026.
- **Root Cause:** In the initial query, the earlier August pass was selected without verifying that the second acquisition date extended past August 26.
- **Remedy:** Ingest the authentic spanning co-event pair on Track 98: `NISAR_L2_PR_GUNW_028_098_A_016_029` (`2026-08-19` to `2026-08-31`). Additionally incorporate Descending spanning pairs: Track 48 (`2026-08-16` to `2026-08-28`) and Track 149 (`2026-08-23` to `2026-09-04`).

### Defect B: Geometry Cross-Contamination in Pre/Post Swipe
- **Pre-Event File:** `rasuwa_nepal_pre.png` (Track 98 Ascending, 2026-08-19, SHA: `80fb4051db0b079dbf843f65b5433ddbfced75272d81cb94ee0deb04612501a2`).
- **Post-Event File:** `rasuwa_nepal_nisar.png` (Assigned Track 98 Ascending in metadata, but derived from Track 48 Descending 2026-11-08, SHA: `2b72a0b5a0c53080d345563309caa757bd561d00e61eae8d3f8166b757214ee7`).
- **Impact:** Ascending satellites illuminate terrain from the West (looking East), while Descending satellites illuminate terrain from the East (looking West). Comparing these directly creates massive false backscatter differences and layover inversion.
- **Remedy:** Download and bundle the genuine Track 98 Frame 16 Ascending GCOV acquisition for `2026-11-11` (`NISAR_L2_PR_GCOV_035_098_A_016_4005_DHDH_A_20261111T233918_20261111T233953_P05036_N_F_J_001`), plus the full post-event series (`2026-09-24`, `10-06`, `10-18`, `10-30`, `11-11`).

### Defect C: Resolution & Quantitative Limitations
- **Current State:** 100×100 px rendered PNG browse thumbnails.
- **Remedy:** Explicitly state in the UI, adapter, and documentation that browse imagery provides qualitative visual comparison only. Provide genuine, direct links to full 2.34 GB HDF5 files on ASF DAAC for calibrated science, and implement a dedicated corridor subsetting pipeline for calibrated quantitative layers.

### Defect D: Inconsistent Documentation & Verification Numbers
- **Current State:** `qa/REPORT.md` stated 19 passed tests, single 1.18 MB chunk, and Hubbard Glacier focus. `README.md` and `SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md` had conflicting claims.
- **Remedy:** Reconcile all documents to reflect the true, newly measured metrics: 45+ passed tests, 4 code-split chunks (<500 KB each), 700 catalogued granules, and the authentic 11-pass Rasuwa series.

### Defect E: Misleading Dialogue in `worldMapData.ts`
- **Current String:** `"How did NISAR detect the flood 7 days before Aug 26?"`
- **Remedy:** Rewrite dialog to explicitly teach why spaceborne radar *cannot* predict sudden floods, contrasting 12-day orbit intervals and pre-event roughness baselines with the need for ground rainfall gauges and community river watch networks.

### Defect F: Official Citation Policy
- **Current String:** `"credit_license": "NASA/JPL-Caltech/ISRO via ASF DAAC. [HUMAN: confirm credit line]"`
- **Official Guidance:** Per Alaska Satellite Facility (ASF) DAAC and NASA ESDIS citation policies:
  *“NISAR data courtesy of NASA/JPL-Caltech and ISRO, distributed by the Alaska Satellite Facility (ASF) DAAC.”*
- **Remedy:** Update all metadata, registry, and UI labels with this authorized citation and cite `asf.alaska.edu/how-to/data-tools/citing-data/`.
