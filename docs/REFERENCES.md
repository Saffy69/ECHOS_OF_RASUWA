## 2. InSAR Principles & SAR Geodesy Literature

9. **Rosen, P. A., Hensley, S., Joughin, I. R., Li, F. K., Madsen, S. N., Rodríguez, E., & Goldstein, R. M. (2000).**
   - *Synthetic aperture radar interferometry.* Proceedings of the IEEE, 88(3), 333-382.
   - Foundational formulation of repeat-pass interferometry, baseline geometry, temporal decorrelation, and phase unwrapping.

10. **Simons, M., & Rosen, P. A. (2007).**
    - *Interferometric Synthetic Aperture Radar Geodesy.* In Treatise on Geophysics (Vol. 3, pp. 391-446). Elsevier.
    - Mathematical treatment of line-of-sight displacement vectors, atmospheric phase screens, and geodetic inversion.

11. **Bürgmann, R., Rosen, P. A., & Fielding, E. J. (2000).**
    - *Synthetic Aperture Radar interferometry to measure Earth's surface topography and its deformation.* Annual Review of Earth and Planetary Sciences, 28(1), 169-209.

12. **Zebker, H. A., & Villasenor, J. (1992).**
    - *Decorrelation in interferometric radar echoes.* IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing, 30(5), 950-959.
