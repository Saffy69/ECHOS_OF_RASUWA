## Voiceover Script (Continuous Prose)
> "When disaster strikes beneath dense monsoon clouds, optical satellites are blind. But Earth's surface never stops dancing. Enter NISAR: NASA and ISRO's L-band radar. We track surface changes across eleven repeat passes at twelve-day orbit cycles. In our interactive supercomputer, drag the swipe wipe to detect slope motion, drape radar observations on 3D terrain, and test physical hypotheses. Transform complex interferometry into an actionable Evidence Request Brief for local community preparedness. Echoes of Rasuwa: Dancing with SAR."
